fxship runs your FiveM server's production and dev copies on one machine and moves changes between
them for you. Your resources live in a GitHub repository. When someone pushes, the dev server loads
the change, checks it, and posts a card in Discord. Nothing reaches your players until someone with
the right role presses **Approve**.

<Steps>

1. **Someone pushes to GitHub**, or a staff member saves a file on the dev server.
2. **Dev loads it live.** Lua files are syntax-checked and dev's console is read for new errors.
3. **A card appears in Discord** with the commits, the resources that change, and the results,
   plus **Approve** and **Reject** buttons only your approver roles can press.
4. **On Approve**, changes that can load live go live on prod at once. Changes that need a restart
   land at your next scheduled restart. **Reject** leaves prod exactly as it is.

</Steps>

## Works with your server as it is

fxship looks at your repository and works out what it needs:

- **Streaming resources**: anything with a `stream/` folder, `data_file` entries or `this_is_a_map`.
- **Core resources** for Qbox, QBCore, ESX, ox_core, ND and vRP, plus database and voice resources.
- **Your layout**: just the `resources` folder in git, or the whole `server-data` folder with
  `server.cfg`.

Anything it gets wrong can be overridden with [one setting](/fxship/reference/configuration/#deploys).
